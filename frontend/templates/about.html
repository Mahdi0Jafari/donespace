The Origin Story

DoneSpace was born out of a real, everyday struggle. My roommate and I wanted our home to be a place of rest, but found ourselves stressed over organizing house chores. We needed a transparent way to keep things clean and live peacefully—without endless arguments over whose turn it was.

The Meal Planning Chaos

Beyond chores, we faced daily meal planning chaos. Every evening, we were hit with the exact same draining question: "What are we cooking?" The lack of a shared schedule left us disorganized, wasting time, and ordering expensive takeout.

The Solution

We desperately needed a unified dashboard to track chores, plan meals, and manage home life effortlessly. When I couldn't find the perfect tool tailored for shared living, I built it myself. That’s how DoneSpace was created: to bring harmony back to your home.
